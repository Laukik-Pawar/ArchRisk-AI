E-commerce platform architecture (demonstration)
The API Gateway depends on Authentication Service, Product Catalog, Cart Service, and Order Service for its customer-facing endpoints.
The Product Catalog depends on Catalog Database and Cache Service.
The Cart Service depends on Cache Service and Inventory Service.
The Order Service depends on Payment Service, Inventory Service, Order Database, and Message Queue.
The Payment Service depends on Payment Provider and Order Database.
The Inventory Service depends on Inventory Database and Message Queue.
The Notification Service depends on Message Queue.
The Authentication Service depends on User Database.
The Message Queue and the databases are infrastructure components. The Payment Provider is an external integration.
Dependencies are directed from consumer to required provider. For this simplified demonstration, every dependency is mandatory, and a failed provider causes its consumers to fail transitively. This intentionally overestimates risk where redundancy or graceful degradation exists.
